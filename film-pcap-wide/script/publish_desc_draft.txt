Your internet traffic travels as numbered envelopes, and anyone on the path can pick them up and read the outside: source, destination, size, timing. This is packet capture — the same trick Wireshark uses, the same trick your ISP's gear can do, the same trick law enforcement needs a warrant for.

This film follows one slow checkout page to a TCP retransmission, shows what a switch sees, what TLS 1.3 actually hides (and what it deliberately leaves in the clear), and where the line sits between debugging and wiretapping.
