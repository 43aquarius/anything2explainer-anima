One network, two couriers. TCP numbers every envelope, waits for receipts, and re-sends whatever gets lost — perfect for a photo, hopeless for a live call. UDP just throws postcards and runs — 8-byte header, no ceremony, late means dead. Your video call uses UDP for the voice and TCP for the signaling, DNS asks one question over UDP, and QUIC rebuilt half of TCP's guarantees on top of UDP itself.
